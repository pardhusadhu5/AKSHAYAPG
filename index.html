Got Questions?

Frequently Asked FAQs

Find answers to common questions about stay guidelines, dining services, and admissions.

Is food included in the monthly rent?

Yes, daily breakfast, lunch, evening tea with snacks, and dinner are fully included in the monthly rental price with no extra cooking or catering charges.

Is high-speed WiFi completely free?

Yes, we provide high-speed, unlimited fiber internet connectivity across all floors and rooms with no separate monthly costs.

Is hot water available 24/7?

Yes, our rooms are supplied with solar water heaters and electric geysers to ensure warm water is available round-the-clock.

Is parking space available for bikes/cycles?

Yes, we have dedicated, secure ground-floor parking for bikes and cycle stands protected by security personnel and CCTV coverage.

Are parents or visitors allowed in rooms?

Parents and siblings are allowed to visit in the common sitting lounge. To maintain safety and privacy for other sharing residents, visitors are not allowed inside bedrooms overnight.

How can I book a room at Dolphin PG?

You can click the "Book a Visit" button to schedule a physical tour. Once you decide, you can complete enrollment by submitting photo ID, biometric inputs, and token advance deposit.

Is laundry facility available?

Yes, we have multiple automated washing machines and a large dedicated drying area on the terrace. Washing cycles are managed seamlessly.
